## 2. Industry aggregates

**2026 capex guidance after Q2 2026 earnings:** Alphabet \$195–205B [Co]; Amazon ≈\$220B cash capex, raised from ≈\$200B on memory costs [P]; Meta \$130–145B incl. finance-lease principal [F]; Microsoft ≈\$175B CY2026 after an accounting change (data-center building lives 15→25 years, more operating leases), underlying plans "unchanged" [P]. Sum ≈ \$720–765B, versus the paper's \$660–690B. Microsoft CY-vs-FY basis needs checking against the transcript.

**Cloud growth (y/y, Q2 2026):** Google Cloud +82% (\$24.8B) [F]; AWS +37% (\$42.2B) [P]; Azure +43% constant currency [P]. Paper cites late-2025 rates of 37% / 24% / 39%.

**Training vs inference:** no authoritative 2026 split found. Deloitte TMT Predictions 2026 remains the main source (inference ≈1/3 in 2023, ≈1/2 in 2025, ≈2/3 in 2026). Epoch AI (2026-05-20, https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/frontier-labs-dont-use-most-ai-compute): OpenAI ≈1.7M H100e (1.9 GW), Anthropic ≈1M (1.4 GW), lab compute growing ≈4×/yr, no split. Nvidia Q2 FY27 (2026-08-26): data-center revenue \$89.0B (+117%), no inference share disclosed [F].

**Timeline beliefs:** Altman (TIME, 2026-08-26) expects an internal system he would call AGI by end-2026 [P]. Amodei: "country of geniuses" in ≈1–3 years (Dwarkesh, early 2026). Hassabis: "2030 ± 1 year" (Axios 2026-05-26, secondary only). LeCun left Meta to found AMI Labs. Metaculus (secondary, Jul 2026): strong AGI median Jan 2033, weak AGI median Jun 2028 [A]. AI Futures Project (2026-08-16): medians shortened slightly (AGI Nov 2028 / Jul 2032) [A]. Net direction in 2026: stable to slightly shorter.

**Discount rates:** Damodaran US WACC file dated 2026-01-05 (https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pc/datasets/wacc.xls): software (system & application) 9.34%, software (internet) 10.66%, semiconductors 10.55%, total market 7.72%. The paper's 7.2% / 10.8% are the January 2025 values. 10-year Treasury: 4.19% (2026-01-02) → 5.28% (2026-10-02) [F].

**AI credit market:** Oracle cut to BBB- by S&P (2026-07-09) on OpenAI concentration, 5-yr CDS ≈200–215 bp [P]. CoreWeave 5-yr CDS ≈855 bp (Jul 2026), 2030 notes yielding ≈11.3–11.5%, Moody's Ba3; its GPU-backed DDTL rated A3 at SOFR+225 [P/A]. Meta Hyperion SPV 2049 notes ≈230 bp over Treasuries (255 bp record in July); El Paso SPV priced >7% [P]. Goldman: ≈\$500B AI-related debt issuance in 2026 YTD [P]. No major AI-lab or neocloud default found through Sep 2026. Leverage sits mainly with compute partners and SPVs, not the labs.

**Power and depreciation:** EIA industrial price ≈9.0¢/kWh (Feb 2026, +8.6% y/y); PJM 2028/29 capacity auction cleared at the \$325/MW-day cap, 6.8 GW short (2026-07-14) [F]. Server book lives: Microsoft/Alphabet 6 yrs, Amazon cut a subset to 5, Meta ≈5.5; debate continues that GPU economic lives are 2–3 years.
